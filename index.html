How to read a deep dive

Trend compares the price with its 50-day and 200-day moving averages — above both usually means an uptrend, below both a downtrend. Volatility is the annualized swing of daily moves: under 20% is calm for a single stock, over 35% is jumpy. 52-week position shows where the price sits inside its last year's range. Deepest slide is the worst peak-to-trough fall of the past year — the patience the stock demanded. Sources: Yahoo Finance price history and headlines, Wikipedia company summaries.
